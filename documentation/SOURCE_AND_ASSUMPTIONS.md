# Source and assumptions register

**Source:** Supplied IABAC CAIBL AI3120 P101 Horizon Retail fictional case and candidate-submitted v1 prioritization matrix, report and pilot charter. Original assessment documents are intentionally not redistributed.

## Case facts
12 stores; annual sales ₹30 crore; first-year AI budget ceiling ₹20 lakh; one pilot. Inventory: 6% item unavailability, ₹45 lakh annual write-offs, 18 months history, 15% stock-count entries missing. Customer service: 8,000 enquiries/month, 10-hour average first response, 60% routine policy enquiries. Marketing: 50,000 records, 2% conversion, consent recorded for 65%. Shelf checks: 2 hours/store/day; no usable image dataset.

## Candidate's submitted judgments (not observed outcomes)
Scores, weights and pilot budget allocations are taken from the submitted working files. They are subjective strategic assessments and indicative allocations, not externally validated performance measures or supplier quotes. The 30% response-delay improvement and 95% reviewed-answer accuracy are proposed pilot targets, not results.

## Derived arithmetic
4,800 routine enquiries/month = 8,000 × 60%. 24 store-hours/day = 12 × 2. Target first response ≤7 hours = 10 × (1 − 30%). These are derived values, not measured outcomes.

## Interpretation cautions
6% unavailability is not 6% lost revenue. Consent recorded for 65% does not establish that the remaining 35% is usable. No customer-level dataset, transactions, trained ML model or completed pilot results exist in this portfolio.
